# Material decoder precision study

This bounded R02 experiment found that the Q4 material decoder configuration
contributes substantially to regular striping on one previously captured input.
Q8 and F16 reduced the selected band and were close to each other. Production
models, generation defaults, geometry, viewer and app protocol were unchanged.

## Controlled comparison

The developer-only `trellis-tex-decode-replay` target calls the existing material
decoder with a saved, already-denormalized latent and four subdivision masks.
No image conditioning, flow, shape decoding, remeshing or UV work is rerun.
The test used one 512-resolution capture, 6,715 latent rows and 2,174,058 material
voxels on CUDA. Q4, Q8, F16 and a repeated Q4 ran serially on the same executable.

Models came from the catalog-pinned `ilintar/trellis2-gguf` revision
`a57397bd3d351599d9729fc144b3f87c3f87d65b`:

| Variant | SHA-256 | Tensor types |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Q4 | `20b208e402db907c6800dd4ad486a0d6e927e4511a6ff87fb51049794988927b` | 66 Q4_0, 40 F16, 178 F32 |
| Q8 | `88b4fced46455e02f316664d5c43584a311921dd9a1cdc1b7b7d981cca9214d4` | 66 Q8_0, 40 F16, 178 F32 |
| F16 | `858d1e0079254e1d1f0596d7fa3a747d21bfe80f83d810e735a1e0b9db550322` | 106 F16, 178 F32 |

All file sizes and hashes matched the catalog. GGUF configuration, tensor names
and shapes matched. Quantizing each of the 66 F16 tensors with `gguf` 0.19.0
reproduced the Q4/Q8 bytes exactly; the remaining 218 tensors were bit-identical.
This establishes a shared converted checkpoint for the precision intervention.
F16 here means the same converted release's reference weights, not an independent
validation against Microsoft's original checkpoint or Python runtime.

Input latent, coordinates, masks and all four expanded coordinate arrays were
bit-identical across variants. Both Q4 raw outputs exactly matched the earlier
full-pipeline capture. Sampling reused the same voxel rows and float32 corner
weights: 609 points along the existing band profile, plus 384,086 projected
surface queries across front/back/left/right views. The Q4 maps reproduced the
earlier native probe maps within 2e-6. Geometry and camera provenance came from
the existing sampling experiment; no new atlas or GLB was produced here.

## Results

The bright/dark points were selected in the earlier experiment and retained,
without selecting new extrema per variant. Luma means `0.2126R + 0.7152G +
0.0722B` on stored colour values, not linear-light luminance.

| Decoder | Selected band contrast | Reduction from Q4 | Surface RGB MAE vs F16 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Q4 | 0.073700 | — | 0.051912 |
| Q8 | 0.030083 | 59.2% | 0.001762 |
| F16 | 0.025864 | 64.9% | 0 |

Four-view inspection showed weaker regular striping with Q8/F16 while retaining
the major painted facet edges. Residual variation remained. The whole image
also became brighter; a mean-subtracted line comparison confirmed the result
was not solely a uniform brightness shift. Colour changes are differences from
F16, not measured error against a ground-truth image. These measurements are
local or screen-sampled; they are not area-weighted quality scores.

The same surface samples had metallic/roughness MAE versus F16 of 0.03281/0.04249
for Q4 and 0.000732/0.002468 for Q8. These material changes justify a later fixed
PBR rendering check. Q8 adds about 35.9 MB to this decoder file, F16 about
103.3 MB relative to Q4; these are file-size differences, not peak VRAM costs.
Decoder times were approximately 7.8–8.5 seconds including trace work. The few
ordered runs are insufficient for a performance recommendation.

Changing model precision also changes backend arithmetic paths. This study
implicates the Q4 decoder configuration as a whole and does not separate weight
rounding from quantized activation/matmul behavior. The latent remains the one
generated by the original pipeline, including its quantized flow. Other inputs,
seeds, backends, full-reference parity and original-source fidelity are untested.

## Fixed-atlas export and PBR follow-up

The subsequent export check is complete. `prepare_fixed_box_replay.py` extracts
native positions, existing UVs, triangle order and the original box bucket from
each face's atlas cell. The research-only `post-replay --fixed-box-mesh` path
reuses the box rasterizer's depth comparison, sparse sampler and gutter dilation
rules without remeshing, decimation or unwrapping. It accepts raw decoder output
and applies the existing scale/clamp. No production caller uses this path.

The Q4 control exactly reproduced the prior export's positions, normals, UVs,
indices and both decoded textures. All Q4/Q8/F16 exports retained that same
139,964-triangle mesh and 1024-square atlas. Every decoded PNG matched the raw
baked RGBA bytes. Diagnostic repetitions reproduced geometry and texture bytes.
All variants used the same original welded/hole-filled BVH and the previous
research **project-first** sampling setting.

| Decoder | Baked contrast at the same saved UV pair | Reduction from Q4 | Area-weighted RGB MAE vs F16 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Q4 | 0.048460 | — | 0.051148 |
| Q8 | 0.016179 | 66.6% | 0.001527 |
| F16 | 0.013380 | 72.4% | 0 |

The band samples use bilinear level-zero PNG sampling at the same 609 saved UV
queries. The area-weighted comparison uses 16 deterministic samples per triangle.
Neither is a ground-truth quality metric. Baking changes the sample values, so
these contrasts should not be treated as identical measurements to the previous
direct volume probes.

All three GLBs imported successfully into Blender 5.1.2 and were inspected in
front/back/left/right views at 512 pixels. PBR uses EEVEE, fixed area lights,
AgX Medium High Contrast and exposure zero. Unlit uses base-colour emission with
Standard/None. Bounds, materials, samplers and recorded renderer settings were
identical within each comparison. Both modes showed weaker striping with Q8/F16;
Q8 was visually close to F16. Some residual pattern and softness remained.
No geometry defect was repaired or newly introduced by the material comparison.

Detailed evidence is retained under ignored `out/material-research/decoder-bake/`:
`RESULTS.md`, exports and raw atlases, `export-validation.json`, per-view renders
and settings, `render-analysis.json`, `baked-line.npz`, comparison charts, commands,
text logs and verified structured diagnostic repeats. This validates these
exports in Blender, not the desktop viewer or other renderers/backends.

## Production-default sampling check

The same three decoder volumes were subsequently baked using direct-then-project
sampling. The executable hash and fixed-mesh bytes matched the preceding study;
only the sampling flag changed. The Q4 control exactly reproduced the earlier
default-sampling geometry and both decoded textures. PNG/array identity,
material/sampler settings, geometry/normals/UVs/indices and renderer settings
were verified again. No new native implementation changes were needed.

| Decoder | Default-sampling baked contrast | Reduction from Q4 | Area-weighted RGB MAE vs F16 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Q4 | 0.031896 | — | 0.051422 |
| Q8 | 0.013480 | 57.7% | 0.001786 |
| F16 | 0.010383 | 67.4% | 0 |

The same 609 UV samples and previously selected pair were used. Four-view PBR
and unlit inspection confirmed weaker striping with Q8/F16; Q8 remained visually
close to F16. The improvement therefore does not depend on the research
project-first sampler on this asset. It is still not a full end-to-end default
generation test: geometry and latent were reused, and encoding remained PNG.

Each variant persisted 41 diagnostic events with no diagnostic errors and a
completed run. Sampling counts matched across precisions: 404,822 direct,
68,607 projected and two shell attempts, with zero missing samples. Attempt
counts include overdraw. Populated atlas coverage also matched. JSONL records
were checked against captured stderr. The original project-first results remain
available for direct comparison.

The same two-point contrast is smaller with default sampling than project-first
even for Q4, while the broader Q4 default profile has pronounced oscillation.
This illustrates why one local pair is insufficient to rank sampling quality.
Residual variation and source-image fidelity remain separate questions.

Evidence is retained under ignored
`out/material-research/decoder-default-sampling/`: `RESULTS.md`, three exports,
raw atlases, `runs.json`, `export-validation.json`, `sampling-comparison.json`,
JSONL/text logs, per-view render settings/images, and within/across-sampler
comparison charts. This step reused the existing tools; real replay, export,
storage, import and render checks passed.

## Mixed-bundle resource check

The candidate can be an application-tested combination of existing upstream
files: keep the Q4 bundle and substitute its material decoder with Q8. No new
training or weight conversion is required. The pinned catalog payload grows
from 6,546,263,488 to 6,582,193,792 bytes: **35.9 MB, or 0.55%**. Using F16 only
for this decoder adds 103.3 MB (1.58%). These are model-file sizes, excluding
download caches, staging and retained installations.

Nine decoder-only replays of the same 512 input, three per precision in rotated
order, completed on the 12 GB CUDA GPU with tracing disabled. Every output
matched its previous precision capture. Median decode times were 8.314 seconds
for Q4, 8.421 for Q8 and 8.304 for F16, with overlapping ranges. This small sample
does not establish a speed regression. Peak process working set remained about
2.19 GB for all three. Private committed bytes are also stored but should not
be confused with resident physical RAM or a minimum RAM requirement.

Whole-device GPU memory was sampled at nominal 100 ms intervals; per-process
GPU allocation was unavailable under Windows. Q4/Q8 peak medians were 6641/6686
MiB, but subtracting per-run preceding idle usage gave 4779/4923 MiB. The differing
45/144 MiB deltas show background noise; neither is a precise decoder memory
increase or supported VRAM minimum. The observed increase was modest on this
machine. Models are loaded/released stage by stage, but activations, allocator
behavior and pipeline state also matter. The full-pipeline peak may be in an
unchanged stage and was not measured in this replay.

Evidence is retained in ignored `out/material-research/decoder-resource-cost/`,
including `RESULTS.md`, `summary.json`, `runs.json`, GPU samples, process memory
samples and raw outputs. A proposed bundle remains unpublished; installed files,
defaults and hardware recommendations are unchanged. Full-pipeline testing at
relevant resolutions and on the intended smaller GPU/backend is needed before
claiming an unchanged minimum requirement. The second-asset check follows.

## Second asset with production sampling

A different previously generated 512 input was captured with the original seed
and settings, using PNG for controlled exports. All 2,583,032 material rows
reproduced the previous saved volume exactly. Q4 decoder replay reproduced the
capture's raw output and both decoded textures. Q4/Q8/F16 reused identical
latent, masks and stage coordinates, with the same verified model files as above.

The three exports share 90,130 vertices, 139,564 triangles, normals, indices,
UVs, a 1024-square atlas, materials and samplers. PNG bytes decode to the exact
baked arrays. Production direct-then-projection sampling gave identical counts:
489,468 direct, 230,030 projected, 15 shell and zero missing attempts. Counts
include overdraw. Each bake stored 41 diagnostic events matching stderr, with
no diagnostic errors and a completed run. The fixed mesh is from this new
capture; final mesh identity with the older generation is not claimed.

| Decoder | Area-weighted RGB MAE vs F16 | Roughness MAE vs F16 | Metallic MAE vs F16 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Q4 | 0.047498 | 0.036635 | 0.031062 |
| Q8 | 0.001839 | 0.002510 | 0.001916 |
| F16 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

Metrics use 16 deterministic samples per triangle, area weighting and bilinear
level-zero texture sampling. They measure F16 agreement, not source fidelity.
The earlier asset's selected stripe pair and reduction percentage do not apply.

All four views were inspected in fixed-light PBR and unlit mode, with identical
recorded renderer settings. Q8 and F16 look closely matched. A purple tint on
Q4 metal regions is largely absent with higher precision. The main material
also becomes greener and some line contrast weakens. Intentional source grain
prevents classifying every softened line as a removed artifact; overall source
fidelity has not been established. Geometry and its softness are unchanged.

Evidence is retained under ignored `out/material-research/decoder-second-asset/`:
`RESULTS.md`, capture, three replays/exports, raw atlases, provenance/commands,
`export-validation.json`, persisted logs, 24 renders with settings and comparison
sheets. The test strengthens Q8's candidacy on a second input; it does not
establish universal improvement, original-reference parity or low-end performance.
Production defaults remain unchanged. Full-pipeline resource validation remains
necessary for a bundle decision, but was parked until final evaluation at the
user's request on 2026-09-13. Other potential pipeline improvements may be
prioritized first through the research backlog.
